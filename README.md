# Anchors & Hourglasses
A mod that adds two new suits to *Balatro* and adds new content surrounding them, ranging from new *poker hands* to new *Jokers*, *Tarot Cards* and more.

## FEATURES:
- 15 new *Jokers*
- 1 new *Deck*
- 2 new *Tarot Cards*
- 4 new *Poker hands*
- 2 new *'Friends of Jimbo'* cosmetic skins
